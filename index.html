Studio

We believe a brand is not a logo.
It is a point of view made visible.

FORMA is a seven-person studio working at the intersection of strategy and craft. We take on a limited number of projects each year — not because we have to, but because depth is the work.

Our clients range from pre-launch founders to established names who want to mean something again.

How we work

01

Immersion

We spend two weeks inside your world before we draw a line. Competitive analysis, audience research, positioning — the foundation that makes everything else inevitable.

02

Diverge

Three distinct directions — not variations of the same idea. Each is argued on its own terms. We present them as propositions, not options.

03

Converge

One direction is chosen and made undeniable. Every detail is pressure-tested: does it work at 8px? At 8 meters? In ten years?

04

System

A brand guide is a promise of consistency. We deliver a living system — documented, extensible, and genuinely usable by your team.
